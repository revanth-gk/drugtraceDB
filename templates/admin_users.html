Enforced by the database

  • CHECK constraints accept only lowercase usernames of 3–30 characters (a-z 0-9 . _ -, starting with a letter) and refuse reserved names.
  • The password_hash column accepts only a bcrypt hash with cost ≥ 12 — plaintext, MD5/SHA or weak hashes are refused.
  • sp_register_login locks an account for 15 minutes after 5 failed sign-ins.
  • Triggers make usernames immutable, stamp password changes (which ends old sessions) and keep at least one recall officer.
