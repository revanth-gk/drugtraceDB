Rules enforced in the transaction

  • INITIAL_RECEIPT: destination only (no source).
  • TRANSFER / RETURN: source and destination, and they must differ.
  • DAMAGE / EXPIRY: source only (stock leaves the network).
  • No future dates; no receiving or transferring an expired batch.
  • Transfers of a batch under an ACTIVE recall are blocked; returns and damage are still allowed.
  • The source must hold enough stock. The ledger is append-only: corrections are new reversing movements.
